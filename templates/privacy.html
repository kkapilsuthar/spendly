Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address and a password (stored in hashed form). While you use Spendly, we also store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to run Spendly: to sign you in, to store and display your expenses, and to produce the summaries and filters you ask for. We do not sell your personal information or use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the Spendly database and is accessible only to your account. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no system is completely secure. You can delete your expenses or your account at any time, and we will remove the associated data.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when a page loads. We do not share your account or expense data with third parties, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact the Spendly team through the support details provided with your account.
